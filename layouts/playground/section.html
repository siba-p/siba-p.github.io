What's being simulated

Overdamped Langevin dynamics in a periodic box (reduced units). Each particle has a gold core and a corona of one DNA strand, either a or a′. Pairs whose strands hybridise feel a Lennard-Jones attraction (cut-off 2.5σ). All other pairs are purely repulsive (WCA), with a core 1.25σ wide to mimic the steric and electrostatic repulsion of non-complementary DNA shells. White lines are hybridised links. "In crystallites" counts particles bonded to at least three partners.

Why the attention lines?

They show what an attention layer does to a particle graph. A query particle scores its neighbours, here by distance plus a bonus for partners it can bind, and a softmax turns the scores into weights. Lower τ focuses attention on a few neighbours. My adhesion model uses the same mechanism to decide which surface sites matter for which polymer beads.
