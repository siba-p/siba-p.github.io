What's being simulated

Overdamped Langevin dynamics of Lennard-Jones particles in a periodic box (reduced units, cut-off 2.5σ). Each frame integrates eight Brownian steps with a cell list. Particles turn gold when they have five or more neighbours within 1.32σ, which is a simple crystallinity criterion. Below kBT ≈ 0.45 the gas condenses into hexagonal crystallites that slowly coarsen.

Why the cyan lines?

They illustrate what an attention layer does to a particle graph. A query particle scores its neighbours, here by distance plus a bonus for being crystalline, and a softmax turns the scores into weights. Lower τ concentrates attention on a few neighbours; higher τ spreads it out. My adhesion model uses the same mechanism to decide which surface sites matter for which polymer beads.
