4.2 The Boltzmann distribution

A system in contact with a heat bath at temperature T is found in a state of energy En with a probability that falls exponentially with energy. Ludwig Boltzmann reached this result in 1877 by counting the number of ways the energy of a gas can be shared among its molecules.

5 Key pointsSelf-check questionsReferences
  1. One page per subject. Each book is a single scrolling page. The contents panel tracks your position and jumps to any chapter or section; on a phone it opens from the button at the top.
  2. Text first. Chapters are continuous prose with numbered sections. They begin with the question that motivated an idea and the people and papers that answered it, then develop the theory step by step.
  3. Equation cards. Key equations are set apart with every symbol explained and a one-line reading in words. Colours in the equation match the colours in the figures.
  4. Interactive figures and code. Figures compute their results as you change the parameters. Code cells run real Python with NumPy in the page; the first run downloads the interpreter, about 10 MB.
  5. End matter. Each chapter closes with its key points, a few questions that explain their answers, and the original papers and textbook chapters for further reading. Chapters can be marked as read; progress is stored only in your browser.
