Sibasankar Panigrahy

Designing programmable nanomaterials, from molecular simulation to machine-learned design.

I study how functionalised nanoparticles organise and interact in complex, crowded environments using all-atom molecular dynamics, free-energy methods and solvation theory. I then build machine-learning models that turn those simulations into design rules for new interfaces.

Research focus

Three questions behind my work

How do nanoparticle surfaces respond to their chemical surroundings, which simulations can we trust to tell us, and how do we turn the answers into designs?
