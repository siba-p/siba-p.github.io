I am a computational materials scientist at QpiAI. My work asks how functionalised nanoparticles behave in crowded, chemically complex environments, and how simulation data can be turned into reliable design rules.

During my PhD at IIT Delhi with Prof. Divya Nayar, I combined all-atom molecular dynamics, Kirkwood–Buff solvation theory and machine learning to study DNA- and citrate-capped gold nanoparticles, and built attention-based networks that design surfaces for a target polymer adhesion.
