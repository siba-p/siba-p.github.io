## About Me

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Materials Science and Engineering at **IIT Delhi**. My research is at the intersection of **Molecular Dynamics (MD) simulations** and **Deep Learning**, specifically focused on predicting complex molecular interactions at interfaces.

---

## Research Focus

Statistical thermodynamics, soft matter physics, and AI-driven materials design. Key areas of my work include:

* **Machine Learning:** Developing Transformer and CNN-based models to predict polymer-surface binding free energies from large-scale MD datasets.
* **Molecular Crowding:** Investigating how amino acid crowders influence the solvation and structure of ss-DNA functionalized gold nanoparticles.
* **Force Field Development:** Re-parameterizing the AMBER99sb-ILDN force-field to prevent artificial aggregation in crowded systems.
