Doctoral Thesis

Modelling and Investigating Macromolecular Crowding effects on Nanomaterial Self-Assembly.

I marry coarse-grained MD with physics-informed neural surrogates to rapidly explore design spaces. The workflow closes the loop between simulation and experiment by exporting fabrication-ready phase diagrams.

  • • Developed a differentiable simulator wrapper with adjoint gradients for parameter sensitivity.
  • • Introduced a contrastive representation for mesoscale structures enabling few-shot classification.
  • • Released OpenCrowdSim dataset with 3M labeled configurations.
